# CASE 037 — CANON

The single source of truth for what happened. Every artifact in the game is
derived from this document and its machine-readable twin, `model.mjs`. If an
artifact contradicts this file, the artifact is wrong, unless `model.mjs` marks
the contradiction as intentional (a lie, a forgery, a clock that is wrong).

Nothing here is shown to players as written. They get records, and the
records are incomplete.

---

## 1. The question underneath the game

**Who gets to decide what happened?**

A record is only as strong as the number of independent people who hold it. In
1994 the case's evidence lived in single places (one tape, one ledger, one
drawing, one order) and a single instruction erased it. In 2024 the same
people tried again, with the evidence spread across three custodians so that no
one instruction could remove it. That design is NEXUS. The three-role team is
the design, not a gimmick: **observation, analysis and verification must each
hold the record independently, and the record is closed only when all three
agree.** The players are the second set of three, brought in because the first
set can be accused of being interested parties.

## 2. Two nights, one building

The campus is the **Admin Building (1974)**, formerly the seat of the **Halder
Institute for Continuity Studies** (dissolved 1998). The Bureau of Continuity
Records, the institute's records office, survives as a retention desk inside the
university. Its standing rule is a **30-year retention** on closed case files.

| | 1994 | 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| Night | 13–14 October 1994 | 17–18 October 2024 |
| What the file says happened | Nothing. No person recovered, none recorded leaving. | Dr. Vey left at 17:30 and has not been seen. |
| What the records show | A figure on a photograph that the tape does not show | Someone in the building after 17:30 |
| Who erased what | One recorder stopped by a writ nobody signed | One camera bank powered down by a ticket nobody signed |
| What was different | One copy of everything | Three custodians, three copies |

The 30-year retention on the 1994 file expired on **14 October 2024**. That is
why the second night happened when it did.

## 4. What happened

  archive and does not return them. Carter's memo re-aims CAM-07 at the corridor

- **13–14 Oct.** Vey, Carter and Rahal run an unauthorised overnight test of
  NEXUS v1 in room **C214-X**, a room that exists on drawing A-201-G issue 02

- **03:12.** Rahal's second camera photographs the corridor, empty.
- **03:16.** CAM-06 (sub-basement) goes offline.
- **03:17.** The Directorate's writ is served: **tape 7 is not to be written
  03:17–03:24.** The operator "did not sign" (recorder log). The only recorder
  stops.
- **03:17:11.** Rahal's handheld camera, which the writ did not reach,
  photographs a figure at the corridor mouth. **This frame is the one record the
  Directorate could not stop**, because it came from a second, independent
  source.
- **03:41.** The entrance is found chained from the inside. Nobody admits to
  chaining it.
- **Afterwards.** Incident Record 037 is written (19 Oct) listing four items;
  only three were printed. The fourth is a person. Vey is withdrawn from the

  the shaft instead of destroying it. The file is closed under 30-year
  retention. Vey spends 1994–98 working off the books in the sub-basement plant,
  where TECH-11 keeps her; she is re-appointed on paper on 17 Apr 1998 with the

### After

Lina Vey is alive. She left through the tunnel at 05:52 and has not been seen
since. On **29 September 2026**, the day before the event, a voicemail arrives on
a Bureau line from a withheld number (41 seconds). It is the last item the team
receives. She is asking for a second quorum, because the first can be accused
of being interested. That second quorum is the three players.

## 5. What NEXUS is

A **records protocol** (one that happens to be built so cleverly that it
looks like intelligence). Its rule: *a fact is closed when three independent
custodians, each holding a separate copy and none able to see the others', agree
on it.* Any single custodian can be pressured, bribed, or ordered to silence.
Three cannot be reached in one instruction.

- **Observation** holds what was seen (photographs, camera frames).
- **Analysis** holds what was measured (ledgers, logs, tapes, timings).
- **Verification** holds what was filed (personnel, incident, custody records).

When Vey says "NEXUS cannot think unless three human hearts provide the pulse",
she means this literally: the protocol does not run without three people.
"They wanted an AI they could weaponise": the Directorate wanted a records
system that would say what it was told. The "entity" the team meets at the end is
the protocol speaking in the first person: a mirror, because it only ever says
back what three people agree is true.

## 6. The ending

The team now holds a closed record (the quorum seal) and knows that the
Directorate's disposal failed. Three things can be done with it, and each is
defensible:

1. **FILE it.** Open the record to the Bureau and the public archive. Risk: the
   people who lied are named. Benefit: nobody can erase it again.
2. **SEAL it.** Keep it closed under the quorum, released only by a later
   quorum. Risk: it stays a secret. Benefit: it cannot be used as a weapon by
   anyone, including its friends.
3. **RETURN it.** Give it back to Vey, who built it and asked for help
   carrying it. Risk: one person holds it again. Benefit: she is the only one who
   knows what it cost.

The game's last entry is the word NEXUS because the choice is the system's
whole nature: three agreeing. What they choose is not recorded.

## 7. Method (how the evidence is built)

- **Evidence families.** Every important fact has several independent
  representations (photograph, log, statement, metadata, object) and at least a
  primary, a secondary and a tertiary path. A team that misses one still has a
  route.
- **Evidence collisions.** Facts become findings when two families collide:
  the bulletin and the 19:45 frame; the badge log and the keypad timing; the
  stopped clock and the café receipt.
- **Reliability is part of the evidence.** A stopped clock is a decoy. A
  disposal order is intent, not action. A badge swipe is a card, not a person.
  Each artifact states what it proves and what it does not.
- **Missing evidence is evidence.** Tape 7 is absent. Page 2 of the writ register is
  absent. The 7th access row is absent. The 04:15–05:15 camera interval is
  absent. Each absence has a reason in this file.
- **Knowledge locks.** The case advances because the team understands, not
  because a box ticks: they must see that the bulletin and the camera cannot both
  be the whole truth before the antenna deck makes sense.
